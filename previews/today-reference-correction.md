# Composed visual discrepancy checklist

Compared the supplied reference against browser captures of the previous release and corrected builds at 390 × 844 and 320 × 568. The attached reference was available in the conversation, but its original image file was not exposed in the workspace. The comparison page displays the captured before-and-after images and accepts the original reference as a local file.

| Region | Previous discrepancy | Correction | Visible differences that remain |
|---|---|---|---|
| Header | Square stacked logo reads as a badge. | Horizontal presentation uses the original SVG mark and lettering from the original light/dark lockups. | Original artwork has its own blue detail and letterforms. |
| Title | The form jumped straight from its question to timing. | Small tracked eyebrow, prominent question, quiet Nigel supporting line. | Heading and spacing tighten at 320px. |
| Timing | Blue-grey track competed with the selected segment. | Ivory bordered track and one muted-blue selected segment. | Plan Ahead reveals the working date controls when selected. |
| Occasions | Unadorned rectangles. | Balanced 2×2 icon tiles with a sun, cup, heart, and briefcase. | Short-height tiles use 44px targets. |
| Impressions | Disclosure read as a left-aligned secondary button. | Compact pills, centered small disclosure, and a chevron. | A selected extra impression remains visible when collapsed. |
| My Style and CTA | Style strip lacked its hanger cue, and CTA lacked directional emphasis. | Compact actual-preference strip with hanger and Edit, followed by one wide arrow CTA. | The strip only appears with a saved preference. It can put the CTA below the first fold at 320px. |
| Pick title | Tall toolbar, then context, then title. | Compact eyebrow, title, and tracked context, with bookmark moved into the photo. | Change styling choices stays available as the back control. |
| Outfit image and rotation | Blue side bands, detached rectangular controls, extra vertical row. | Warm photo stage, full-body contain sizing, circular controls inside the stage, and numbered angle label. | The supplied photos keep their native aspect ratios. Warm gutters may remain. |
| Actions | Detached controls and taller spacing delayed the next action. | Primary button under the photo, icon-led refinements immediately below. The same primary button docks when off-screen on a short phone. | Plan status and actual wear reveal their existing context-specific controls. |
| Garment strip | Large separate heading and tall tiles. | Tighter horizontal thumbnails directly below actions. | Names remain visible and can wrap. |
| Advice | Separate title treatment with no in-panel disclosure. | Restrained tinted disclosure, open by default, then practical wear instructions. | Advice can continue below the fold on smaller viewports. |

The personal and public builds use the same layout and controls. Personal photography and the public mannequin differ by design.
