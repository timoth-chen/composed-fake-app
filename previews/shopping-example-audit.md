# Shopping example audit

The shared rubric uses close, partial, and weak matches for each photographed product. The match tier comes from the candidate’s colour, silhouette, and design against the chosen aesthetic. Illustrative percentages are separate from wardrobe overlap and personal fit. No preference and custom styles without a rubric have no number.

|---|---|---|---:|---|---|

| Personal | Olive cotton overshirt | East Asian Minimalist | 92% | I’d skip it | Muted olive and a straight silhouette match well. The Barbour already covers much of the casual layering role. |
| Personal | Stone straight trousers | East Asian Minimalist | 70% | Worth trying on | Quiet colour fits, while the tailored shape differs from more relaxed styling. Stone provides a lighter option alongside black trousers and dark indigo pleats. |
